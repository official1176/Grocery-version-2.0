SHOPDESK — GROCERY RETAIL MANAGEMENT

GET STARTED
1. Open index.html in a recent desktop browser such as Chrome or Edge.
2. Open Shop settings and enter your shop name, address, phone, GSTIN, state,
   default GST rules and cashier name. Use product-level GST rates where needed.
3. Add products in Products & stock, or open Import items to scan a barcode or
   import a CSV / Excel .xlsx workbook. The workbook’s first sheet is used.
   Enter your own prices and stock; barcodes do not fill prices automatically.
4. Open New sale. Search by name, use grocery category filters and quick-add
   buttons, or scan a barcode with a USB/Bluetooth scanner or the phone camera.
5. Complete the sale with cashier, optional customer name and phone, payment
   amount and method. The app calculates GST and balance due and saves the bill.
6. Open Sales invoices to view a bill by date or find previous customer bills.
   Print an open bill with F8, or collect a balance due later.
7. Open Monthly reports or GST & tax to review saved invoice totals for a month.
8. In WhatsApp, open the official WhatsApp Web account and use saved customer
   phones to start chats or share a prefilled bill message.
9. In Tally export, enter exact existing ledger names, choose a month and
   download XML vouchers. In TallyPrime, use Import Data → Transactions.

FAST BILLING KEYS
- F1: show all shortcuts.
- F2: focus the product search or barcode field.
- F3: open payment and focus the customer name.
- F4: focus the first bill quantity.
- F5 / F6 / F7: choose Cash / UPI / Card in payment.
- F8: print the open bill or last saved receipt.
- F9: open payment for the current bill.
- Ctrl+Enter: complete the sale from the payment form.

FEATURES
- Retail overview with sales trends, inventory valuation, stock health, GST,
  payment totals, category demand, recent bills and top products.
- Product catalog with owner-set prices, voice-to-text item names where the
  browser supports speech input, decimal quantities, grocery categories,
  optional barcodes, low-stock alerts and stock movement history.
- CSV and Excel .xlsx import with validation, duplicate barcode skipping,
  opening-stock movement records, CSV template and catalog export.
- Barcode item lookup: scan a saved item to edit stock/price, or add an unknown
  barcode and enter its product name, selling price and stock.
- Sales screen with best-seller-first quick-add buttons, grocery category filters,
  product search and optional phone-camera scanning.
- Automatic daily bill numbering; cashier, customer, payment and GST details are
  saved with each invoice.
- Partial payments, balance due tracking and later payment collection.
- Sales invoices by date, bill, customer name, phone or cashier; daily CSV export.
- Monthly sales and collection reports with daily totals and product rankings.
- GST detail summary by rate, taxable value, CGST, SGST and IGST.
- Shop details for name, category, phone, email, GSTIN, state, PIN, address,
  currency, default GST setup and receipt footer.
- WhatsApp Web handoff, customer chat links and prefilled bill sharing. ShopDesk
  does not read WhatsApp messages or import WhatsApp contacts.
- Tally XML export for accounting sales and receipts using configured ledgers.
  It does not create Tally stock items or update Tally inventory.
- 80 mm bill printing with F8 shortcut, plus database backup and restore.

DATA
ShopDesk stores its database in the browser on this device. It works offline
once opened, but shop records do not sync to another device or browser. Download
a backup before changing devices or clearing browser data. Camera scanning needs
browser permission and a secure page context (HTTPS or localhost). If camera
scanning is unavailable, use a USB/Bluetooth keyboard-style scanner or type the
barcode. Excel .xls workbooks are not supported; save as .xlsx or .csv. Tally
ledger masters must exist in the selected company before importing the XML.
